voice health user-manual 
1. Introduction
Purpose
VoiceHealth is a voice-based health advisory application designed to assist caregivers and community members by providing general health information through voice and text interactions.
The application supports:
•	Voice questions
•	Text questions
•	Spoken responses
•	Emergency symptom detection
•	Offline health advice
________________________________________
2. Important Safety Notice
VoiceHealth provides general health information only.
The application:
•	Does not diagnose diseases.
•	Does not replace qualified medical personnel.
•	Should not be used as the sole source of medical decision-making.
If a danger sign is detected, users must immediately seek professional medical assistance.

4. Logging In
1.	Open VoiceHealth.
2.	Enter email address.
3.	Enter password.
4.	Select Log In.
New users may register before logging in.
________________________________________
5. Asking a Health Question
Voice Input
1.	Open Voice Query.
2.	Press the microphone button.
3.	Speak clearly.
4.	Wait for processing.
5.	Review the advice.
Example:
"My child has fever and diarrhea."
________________________________________
Text Input
1.	Enter a health question.
2.	Press Analyze.
3.	Read the generated advice.
________________________________________
6. Understanding Advice
Every response may contain:
Topic
The identified health issue.
Advice
General health guidance.
Tips
Recommended self-care actions.
See a Doctor If
Conditions requiring professional care.
Source
The organisation or guideline used.
________________________________________
7. Emergency Symptoms
VoiceHealth automatically identifies danger signs.
Examples include:
•	Difficulty breathing
•	Convulsions
•	Unconsciousness
•	Severe bleeding
•	Severe chest pain
•	Stroke symptoms
When detected:
1.	An urgent warning is displayed.
2.	Spoken emergency advice is provided.
3.	The user is instructed to seek medical care immediately.
________________________________________
8. Offline Operation
VoiceHealth supports offline health advice.
Available offline:
✅ Local knowledge base
✅ Emergency symptom rules
✅ User history
✅ Previously loaded content
The application continues operating even when internet access is unavailable.
________________________________________
9. Spoken Advice
VoiceHealth can read advice aloud.
To enable:
1.	Open Settings.
2.	Enable "Read Advice Aloud".
________________________________________
10. Health Information Sources
All advice is derived from reviewed health information sources.
Each knowledge item includes:
•	Source reference
•	Health category
•	Related condition
This supports the application's content governance process.
________________________________________
11. Troubleshooting
Speech Not Working
•	Check microphone permission.
•	Move to a quieter environment.
•	Try speaking more clearly.
No Advice Returned
•	Rephrase the question.
•	Use simple symptoms.
•	Check spelling.
No Sound
•	Increase device volume.
•	Enable text-to-speech.
________________________________________
